About Me

I am an EPSRC Postdoctoral Fellow at the Mathematical Institute, University of Warwick, UK, hosted by Prof. Martin Lotz. Previously, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Colorado Boulder, where I worked under the supervision of Prof. Joshua Grochow. I obtained my PhD in May 2020 from Purdue University, supervised by Prof. Saugata Basu and Prof. Elena Grigorescu.

Very broadly, I am interested in algebraic methods (interpreted widely), sometimes inspired by mathematical applications. Some specific interests are in (topological aspects in) real algebraic geometry and o-minimal geometry, with connections to areas such as combinatorics.

I previously obtained a master's degree from Brown University, USA, and a bachelor's degree from R. V. College of Engineering, India.

In 2025, I organized the conference OMIGAWD: O-Minimal Geometry - Interactions, Applications and Wider Developments (2025), supported by my EPSRC grant and a grant from the University of Warwick's MRC.
